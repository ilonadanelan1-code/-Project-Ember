# Tactical Controls — Project Ember

## Touch-first controls

- Friendly unit tap: select one unit.
- `ДОБОР` + friendly unit tap: add or remove a unit from the current selection.
- Drag-selection rectangle: select friendly units whose screen positions are inside the rectangle.
- Selected units + enemy tap: focus-fire the tapped enemy.
- Selected units + ground tap: move to the selected formation layout. With `МАРШ` enabled this becomes attack-move.
- `ВСЕ`: select the entire player army.
- `ДЕРЖАТЬ`: defend current positions and attack enemies that enter weapon range.
- `СТОП`: cancel current orders.
- `ОТСТУП`: withdraw selected troops toward their deployment edge.
- Formation button cycles line, column and wedge.
- Turn on **ЗАПИСЬ** and tap **ГР. 1–4** to save the current selection to a group; tap a saved group button again to recall it. This works without a keyboard.
- Zoom buttons change the orthographic camera distance. A second finger drag pans the battlefield; magnify gestures zoom when emitted by the device.

## Keyboard controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| A | Toggle attack-move orders |
| H | Hold selected units |
| S | Stop selected units |
| Escape | Clear selection |
| R | Restart isolated battle |
| Arrow keys | Pan battlefield |
| Ctrl+1…4 | Assign selected units to a control group |
| 1…4 | Recall a control group |

## Unit roles

- **Swordsman:** durable general-purpose melee infantry.
- **Archer:** ranged damage, prefers to maintain distance, low armor.
- **Spearman:** armored front rank, extended reach and bonus damage against cavalry.
- **Cavalry:** fast flanking unit with a charge strike after a fast approach; charge has a cooldown.

## Formation intent

- **Line:** spearmen form the leading rank, swordsmen support, cavalry occupies wings, archers remain behind.
- **Column:** concentrates units into a narrow marching column.
- **Wedge:** places the spear/sword group into a forward wedge, cavalry on the flanks and archers at the rear.
