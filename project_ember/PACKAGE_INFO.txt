Project Ember v0.19.0 — Combat Animation Pass
Source project ZIP for Godot 4.7.2 stable / GL Compatibility.
The owner's integrated build includes the private Tiny Swords combat atlas alongside the v0.18.1 hex terrain system, battle animation integration, tests and QA scripts.
Melee, archer and spear units use animated pixel-art sprites; cavalry retains its original mounted model. Combat calculations, collision, AI, unit stats and saves are unchanged.
Not an APK. Open project.godot in Godot 4.7.2. First import may take a little longer while the atlas texture is imported.
