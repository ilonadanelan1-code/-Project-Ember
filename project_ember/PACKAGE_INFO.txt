Project Ember v0.19.2 — Melee Contact Hotfix
Godot 4.7.2 stable / GL Compatibility source project for Android.

Includes the complete v0.19.1 project and locally integrated Tiny Swords combat atlas.

Fixes melee units failing to land valid hits: melee units now enter the wind-up at their original role attack range. When a strike begins, its hit remains committed until the weapon-contact frame, unless the target dies or is removed. The previous extra contact-distance recheck no longer silently discards moving targets.

Animation-timed melee contact, bow release, target-facing, guard/defence recovery and corpse checks remain enabled. Unit stats, damage, cooldowns, armor, AI, collision and saves are preserved.

Not an APK. Open project.godot in Godot 4.7.2 Android Editor and allow first import to finish.
