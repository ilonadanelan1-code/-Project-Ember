Project Ember v0.19.1 — Combat Readability Pass
Godot 4.7.2 stable / GL Compatibility source project for Android.

The integrated owner ZIP includes the Hex Terrain Bundle and a locally generated Tiny Swords combat atlas. Swordsmen, archers and spearmen use animated pixel-art units; cavalry retains its mounted renderer.

Melee damage is applied at the weapon-contact frame. Arrows are spawned at bow release. Units face their current targets, ignore dead/corpse targets, cancel unfinished actions on new orders, and use guard/defence poses between attacks. Lancer proportions are adjusted without stretching the long pike.

Unit stats, damage values, attack cooldowns, armor, AI, collision and saves are preserved. Public source CI omits the separately licensed sprite atlas and tests the fallback renderer.

Not an APK. Open project.godot in Godot 4.7.2 Android Editor and allow the first import to finish.
