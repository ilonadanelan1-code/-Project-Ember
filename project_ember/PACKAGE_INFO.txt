Project Ember v0.31.0 — AI Neutral Site Expeditions
Godot 4.7.2 stable / GL Compatibility source project for Android landscape. This is not an APK.

Feature summary:
- All nine AI kingdoms can dispatch deterministic expeditions to nearby neutral sites when not pursuing an active war target.
- A living AI kingdom reserves one target at a time; other living kingdoms avoid that reservation.
- AI armies approach a location to an adjacent hex and resolve a bounded strategic battle using their current troop health, veterancy, role damage and armor.
- Defeated guards and guardian health persist. AI victories record the conquering kingdom and capture turn, deposit the location reward into the nearest AI city and apply the site's troop or economic effect.
- Abandoned-mine production is paid only to the owner of the mine. Player and AI mine income are separated.
- The strategic map reports which AI kingdom cleared a site; cleared sites cannot be attacked again by the player.
- War targets retain priority over neutral expeditions.

Compatibility:
- SAVE_VERSION remains 1. Older cleared sites without an owner are treated as player-cleared; new ownership and target fields are optional and normalized.
- No new resource, unit role, building ID, kingdom ID or location ID is introduced.
