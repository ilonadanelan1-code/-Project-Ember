Integrated features:
- SettlementCatalog centralizes city/outpost rules, costs, upkeep, influence and AI reserves.
- CampaignState stores outposts separately, supports founding, collection, annexation and conversion to cities, and includes optional persistence fields.
- TerritoryCatalog calculates city > outpost > field-army influence for player and AI without persisting an owner grid.
- KingdomAI and StrategicAIPlanner account for outpost income, upkeep, threat and frontier expansion; AI can annex nearby outposts.
- StrategyMap provides city/outpost foundation selection, map markers, an outpost panel, resource collection and annexation/upgrade controls.
- A new settlement_economy_smoke_test.gd covers costs, territory, founding, save/load, collection, player and AI annexation, AI reserve protection and legacy defaults.
- SAVE_VERSION remains 1; existing game systems and the Tiny Swords pixel-art atlas are retained.
