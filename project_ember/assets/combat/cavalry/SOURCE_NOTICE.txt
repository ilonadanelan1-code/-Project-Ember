Mounted horse sprites were copied from the user-supplied Full_Pack.zip.
The uploaded archive did not contain a README or license file, and its author/source could not be identified from the archive contents.
The original riding, rearing and jump sheets are retained in this folder. The runtime reads normalized blue_animations.png and red_animations.png atlases generated by tools/assets/build_cavalry_atlases.py. Riding frames are cropped on the verified 80x64 grid (11 idle frames and 8 gallop frames); rearing and jump sheets are separately normalized to consistent 88x82 runtime frames with a stable ground anchor.
