PROJECT EMBER v0.38.1 — AI EXPANSION STABILITY FIX

Source basis: Project Ember v0.38.0 (full playable project from upstream game branch release/v0.36.1).

Problem addressed:
- AI army target previously grew toward 10 units with only one capital. With a market and army upkeep, net income could settle around zero before the treasury reached the settlement expansion reserve. The AI then retained its starting city for very long campaigns.
- City/building/recruitment orders were queued before a due settlement foundation, so the AI could spend the same gold that its expansion planner had reserved.

Changes:
- Early desired army size now starts from 5 units and scales with additional cities/population; war and threats retain their defensive bonuses.
- Expansion reserve permits net gold down to -1 when the total treasury can afford the foundation and multiple upkeep turns.
- Due foundations are resolved before the AI queues purchases and recruitment.
- AI city cost, minimum population and city cap are centralized in SettlementCatalog. City spacing now uses the shared configured value.
- Added a 180-turn, peaceful multi-kingdom regression simulation to strategic_ai_smoke_test.gd.
- Player settlements, outposts, annexation, resource collection, territory hierarchy, UI, and SAVE_VERSION 1 are retained.

Local validation:
- Python static QA: 233/233 checks passed.
- Python syntax compilation: tools/qa/static_validate.py passed.
- Playable release package script validates the Tiny Swords 1152x2304 RGBA atlas, animation manifest, required project files and ZIP integrity.
