Project Ember v0.9.1 — Units
- Each unit now uses two merged vertex-coloured ArrayMesh batches: static body silhouette and an animated action part.
- Ten cached team/role mesh combinations share a single vertex-colour material.
- Distinct silhouettes and role details are retained for swordsmen, archers, spearmen, cavalry and commanders.
- Added idle/movement animation, melee swings, spear thrusts, bow draw/release, cavalry charge motion and commander banner motion.
- Added shared-material hit flash, falling death animation and quality-capped 8–10 second corpses.
- Selection/target feedback uses one cached ring; health bars appear only when selected, targeted or wounded.
- Core BattleUnit node count is capped at eight including collision, ring and health elements; CI tests 50 unit instances.
- Campaign saves, unit role IDs, damage formulas, speeds, formation slots, collision and input mapping remain unchanged.
- Godot 4.3 GL Compatibility is retained; real Android performance, touch rendering and combat readability still require device testing.
