Project Ember v0.19.3 — Attack Command Reliability
- Explicit attack commands clear stale attack cooldowns and immediately enter ATTACK mode.
- Separation steering now considers living same-team units only, not the enemy target.
- Reduced separation influence so clustered allies do not overpower the pursuit vector.
- Direct attack orders make archers fire when the target is inside attack range even when it is closer than their preferred range.
- Adds regression tests for attack-order activation, forward pursuit, cooldown reset and close-range archer fire.
- Preserves unit statistics, damage, cooldowns, armor, AI, collisions, projectiles, animations and save data.
