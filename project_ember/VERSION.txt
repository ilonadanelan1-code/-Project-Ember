Project Ember v0.19.2 — Melee Contact Hotfix
- Restores original melee attack range for the start of each wind-up.
- Keeps a started melee hit committed through its contact frame; the hit is cancelled only if the target dies or is removed.
- Prevents slight target movement during the animation wind-up from silently cancelling a valid strike.
- Adds a regression test for a target moving during melee contact timing.
- Preserves role stats, damage, cooldowns, armor, AI, collision, save data, sprite animations and ranged projectile release.
